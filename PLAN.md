# Le C. (LE C. Coiffeur Coloriste) — Research & Plan

> **Le C.** is a small, premium **coiffeur-coloriste** at **3 rue du Chapitre, 35000 Rennes**, in the medieval heart of the old town near the Cathédrale Saint-Pierre. Its own logo and Planity text write the name **"LE C."**, with the full stop. **Magali** (Magali Martinskevitch, the gérante) founded it and opened in **2020**, « quinze jours avant le Covid ». She spent **11 years as a L'Oréal Professionnel trainer** and is a **Redken-certified colourist**. The salon is built around time and ritual. Every package starts with a **« Diagnostic personnalisé »**, then a **shampooing traitant**, a **rituel massant** and a **soin personnalisé**, before the cut and styling. Magali told 7 Jours the team spends **1h to 1h15 per client**, about double the usual time. Colour is the core: natural **balayages**, **« Blonds Ultimes »**, copper and violet work, **grey blending**, and organic plant colour as a **Couleurs Gaïa** partner of five years, with refill products sold **en vrac**. The salon says it uses only premium hair products that are **« VEGAN et CRUELTY FREE »**. The room is a half-timbered stone house with exposed beams, high ceilings and an old fireplace. Inside are dark teal walls, terracotta hexagonal floor tiles, a pink cherry-blossom tree, a black-and-white toile mural, Edison bulbs and gilded birdcages, all behind a **deep-blue door**. Clients call it « cosy », « style appartement », « ambiance atelier d'artiste », « pas aseptisée ». It is rated **5.0/5 from 201 Planity reviews**, books only through **Planity**, and has **no website of its own**. This site would be its first.

## 2. Story & history

**The founder.** Magali Martinskevitch was a trainer for L'Oréal for 11 years (« 11 ans formatrice #lorealprofessionnel ») and calls herself « certifiée coloriste monde #redken ». 7 Jours (05/09/2024, Adélaïde Hasle) presents her as « gérante du C coiffeur, rue du Chapitre à Rennes ». She opened in 2020, « quinze jours avant le Covid ! ». A Facebook mention (search snippet only) congratulates « Magali Martinskevitch pour l'ouverture du salon de coiffure Le C Coiffeur Coloriste à Rennes : 3 rue du Chapitre » (https://www.facebook.com/leccoiffeur/mentions).

**The timeline (verified):**
- **11 Dec 2019**: company LE C (sigle LC) created as an **SAS**; Magali is Présidente from 17 Dec 2019.
- **23 Jan 2020**: activity starts at 3 rue du Chapitre (register).
- **Early 2020**: the salon opens « quinze jours avant le Covid » (founder, 7 Jours). The exact opening day is not published. **Write « depuis 2020 ».**
- **About 2021**: Couleurs Gaïa partnership begins (« Partenaire depuis 5 ans » on the 2026 page; the start year is an estimate).
- **1 Jun 2022**: the company becomes an **SARL** « gérée par Mme Magali MARTINSKEVITCH, associée unique »; she is gérante from 2 Jul 2022.
- **5 Sep 2024**: 7 Jours article. It cites the salon as an example of the move toward the haircut as a self-care moment that clients will pay a premium for, and describes a "4-star" service.
- **Feb 2026**: recruiting BP apprentices for June or September 2026.
- **Mar 2026**: Davroe gift sets arrive (« ✨ Les coffrets Davroe sont enfin là ✨ »).
- **Oct 2026**: 5.0/5 from 201 Planity reviews.

**The philosophy, in her words (7 Jours):**
- « Nous passons entre 1h et 1h15 par cliente » (about double the 30-minute mid-range average).

## 9. Design direction

Skills consulted: `ui-ux-pro-max:ui-ux-pro-max` (design-system search: "hair salon colourist heritage editorial", variance 7 / motion 8 / density 3; typography search: "elegant editorial serif luxury") and `design-taste-frontend`. The ui-ux-pro-max generic output (Swiss minimalism, Outfit + Work Sans, black with a hot-pink #EC4899 accent) was **rejected**: it is a template that ignores this salon's house, door and mural. Its useful parts are kept: complex-tier motion with Flip-style shared elements, reduced-motion final states, a spacious density scale, and a 4.5:1 CTA contrast check. From `design-taste-frontend`: the design read, dials, one locked accent, one shape rule, no em-dashes in site copy, a max of one marquee, eyebrow restraint, no scroll cues, and the ban on the beige + brass "premium" palette (this palette comes from the real door, walls and tree instead).

**Design read:** a heritage-house salon site for Rennes women (and men) choosing a colourist they can trust. It speaks the language of an engraved toile coming into colour, and leans toward native CSS plus GSAP scroll storytelling on Vite vanilla-ts.

**Dials:** DESIGN_VARIANCE **7** · MOTION_INTENSITY **8** · VISUAL_DENSITY **3** (airy, but the price pages are deliberately denser, at about 5).

**Sibling note:** another Rennes hair salon project lives in this workspace (`Nature-de-cheveux-Rennes`). This direction must stay clearly distinct from it. The sibling sites already use Gloock, Bodoni Moda, Schibsted Grotesk, Archivo, Big Shoulders, Instrument Sans and Barlow, so none of those are used here.

### Concept: **« Toile & Balayage »**
*An ink-engraved house on rue du Chapitre that a colourist paints, one streak at a time.*

Two real things in the salon drive it:
- the **black-and-white toile mural**, the black-on-white logo and sign, and the engraved feel of the half-timbered house;
- **colour as the craft**: balayage streaks, foil sections, copper, violine and blonde.

So the site begins in **ink on white**: line-engraved original illustrations of the façade, the beams, the fireplace, the blossom tree. **Colour enters only through the colourist's gestures.** Streaks sweep diagonally across an engraving like a balayage. Foil-strip wipes carry you between pages. Every colour comes from the real room: door blue, blossom pink, tomette terracotta, lantern gold.

### Personality keywords
chaleureux · atelier d'artiste · sur mesure · patient (« on prend le temps ») · expert sans être froid · pas aseptisé · éthique · vieille pierre, gestes précis

### Motion language
Stack: Vite vanilla-ts. **GSAP** (ScrollTrigger, SplitText; check the current version and licence in the official docs first) for pinning and scrubbing; **Lenis** for smooth scroll; **CSS scroll-driven animations** (`animation-timeline: view()`) for light reveals, with an IntersectionObserver fallback (check caniuse); the **View Transitions API** for cross-document page transitions (`@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`, checked on caniuse, with a plain-navigation fallback). Animate transform, opacity and clip-path/mask only. No `scroll` event listeners.

- **Hero (about 2.2 s, once per session):** the engraved façade **draws itself** (SVG stroke-dashoffset), hatch lines last. Then three **balayage streaks** sweep diagonally (mask-position), colouring the door bleu-chapitre and the blossom cerisier. In dark mode the windows warm to lantern gold. The headline splits by line (SplitText) and rises 12px with opacity. Skip on repeat visits within the session.
- **Scroll storytelling: « Le Rituel » (pinned, `start: "top top"`):** six real steps (Diagnostic personnalisé → Shampooing traitant → Rituel massant → Soin personnalisé → Coupe → Coiffage / brushing), each its own engraved vignette. A thin **clock ring** fills as you scroll: the 30-minute mark is passed and the ring keeps going to « 1 h – 1 h 15 », with Magali's quote « Nous passons entre 1h et 1h15 par cliente » landing at the end. **Critic pass, grounded detail:** the « Shampooing traitant » and « Rituel massant » vignettes should show what clients actually describe: **a reclining wash chair with a padded neck rest** (« le siège et le support molletonné pour le cou », « le shampoing allongé », « 1ere fois que je suis allongée sans avoir mal au cou »), the head massage that « dure ». Pair each step with one short real quote from §4 (#19, #20, #23). No sound anywhere on the site, as a nod to « à la différence des salons bruyants avec radio commerciale ».
- **« La Couleur » nuancier:** horizontal pan (pinned wrapper, scrubbed track, `end: "+=" + distance`). Each swatch's strand **lightens from root to tip** as it crosses the centre, which reads as a balayage.
- **« La Maison »:** gentle parallax between the beam layer and the room layer (max 40px). The blossom tree's petals drift only when that section is in view, in small numbers.
- **Page transitions: « la feuille d'alu »:** a vertical foil strip (bleu-chapitre in light mode, ardoise in dark mode) folds across the screen and away, as a colourist folds a foil, about 550 ms with an `expo.inOut`-like ease. The shared element (the arch window) morphs between pages through `view-transition-name`.
- **Micro-interactions:** buttons fill with a **colour wash entering from the pointer's side** (directional hover); press = `scale(0.98)`. Length tabs snap with a short spring. Price numbers roll on change. The lantern toggle flickers once on switch. Links get a hand-drawn underline that draws in. Focus rings stay visible (2px cerisier-fonce or cerisier, with an offset).
- **One marquee only:** on the home page, a slow line of the salon's own words (« diagnostic personnalisé · rituel massant · sur mesure · écoute · douceur · végétal · VEGAN et CRUELTY FREE »). It pauses on hover and stops under reduced motion.
- **Reduced-motion fallback** (`prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`): engravings appear already drawn and already coloured; no pinning (the ritual becomes a stacked list with the clock ring shown full); the nuancier becomes a static scroll-snap row; page transitions become a 150 ms cross-fade or nothing; no parallax, petals, marquee or balayage pointer effect; the length gauge still works and prices change instantly.
